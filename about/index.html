The studio

Built like the work:small, sharp, deliberate.

ORVIQO exists because ambitious companies kept having to choose between agencies that design and agencies that build. We refused the choice.

The story

ORVIQO began in 2024 with an observation: the internet had never been louder, and the brands people actually trusted had never been quieter. Restraint had quietly become the rarest thing on the web — and the most valuable.

So we built a studio around it. One team where strategy, design and engineering happen at the same table, so nothing gets lost between the idea and the shipped page. We design in the browser's reality. We set performance budgets before moodboards. We treat AI as plumbing, not fireworks.

The name carries the idea: inside ORVIQO is an orb — a body of light that doesn't chase attention, and holds it anyway. That's the standard every project here is held to.

How we're built

Three commitments, kept.

Small is the strategy

ORVIQO stays deliberately small: a senior bench, no account-manager relay, no juniors learning on your invoice. Every project gets the people whose names are on the door.

Worldwide by default

Based in Vadodara, shipping everywhere. Our clients span nine countries and most of them we've never met in person — just in launches, numbers, and long-running Slack channels.

Taste is a discipline

We believe restraint can be engineered. Every ORVIQO project passes the same private review: if a section feels ordinary, it gets redesigned before you ever see it.

The standard

One team, no handoffs

Strategy, design and engineering sit at the same table from day one. Nothing is lost in translation because there is no translation.

Performance is aesthetic

A site that hesitates feels cheap, whatever it looks like. We treat speed as a design material — budgets are set before the first pixel.

Craft compounds

We build systems, not snapshots. Every token, component and rule is made to still be useful when your business is three times its size.
